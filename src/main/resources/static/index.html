How it works

  1. 1. Upload your PDF

    Choose the document you want signed.

  2. 2. Add the signers

    Their email addresses, in order or in any order.

  3. 3. Everyone signs

    Each person signs on their own computer, with their own card.

Nothing is signed in the browser. Signing happens in the SignFlow app on your computer, with your card in the reader.

You keep the key. The certificate stays on the card. This website never sees it, and neither does anyone else.
